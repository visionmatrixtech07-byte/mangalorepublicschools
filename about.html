About Mangalore Public Pre University College, Hassan

Mangalore Public Pre University College is a unit of the Mangalore Education & Charitable Trust and is affiliated to the Karnataka Pre-University Education Board. Our motto is "College of Excellence".

What we offer

We offer Science (PCMB and PCMC) and Commerce (CEBA) for students in Hassan and across Hassan district, including Belur, Arsikere, Channarayapatna, Sakleshpur, Holenarasipura, Alur and Arkalgud. Students from other taluks can use our hostel.

Science

PCMB and PCMC for medicine, engineering and technology.

Commerce

CEBA for CA, CS, CMA, B.Com and BBA.

Campus and hostel facilities

College

  • Well furnished classrooms with a good study environment
  • Well equipped laboratories
  • Digital boards for effective teaching and speedy revision
  • Library with a large collection of books
  • Well equipped auditorium
  • Lift facility for parents of students
  • Canteen and stationery availability

Hostel

  • Well furnished, comfortable rooms and experienced wardens
  • Surveillance cameras and 24x7 security guards
  • Vegetarian food with a clean, hygienic dining area
  • Weekly once phone facility for students
  • Sunday entertainment and cultural events, festival celebrations
  • One day trip to a historic place of Karnataka
