About Mangalore Public Schools, Hassan

Mangalore Public Schools is a unit of the Mangalore Education & Charitable Trust. We teach children from LKG to Class 10 in Hassan.

What we offer

We teach LKG, UKG and Classes 1 to 10 for children in Hassan and across Hassan district, including Belur, Arsikere, Channarayapatna, Sakleshpur, Holenarasipura, Alur and Arkalgud. Ask us about the school van for your area.
