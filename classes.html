LKG to Class 10 in Hassan

Each stage has its own teachers and its own pace. Class details and fees are confirmed at admission.

Primary: LKG, UKG and Classes 1 to 4

Children learn reading, writing and numbers through activity, stories and play. Teachers know every child by name, and parents get regular updates.

Middle school: Classes 5 to 7

Stronger basics in languages, maths and science, with regular tests and homework habits that build confidence.

High school: Classes 8 to 10

Board exam preparation with regular practice tests, revision on digital boards and doubt-clearing by subject teachers.

Admission documents

  • Birth certificate
  • Previous school’s transfer certificate (for Class 1 and above)
  • Aadhaar
  • Passport-size photographs
