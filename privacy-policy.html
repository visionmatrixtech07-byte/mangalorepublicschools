Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What this website collects

This website does not have a login or a database. If you use the enquiry form, the details you type (child’s name, class and parent phone number) are placed in a WhatsApp message that you choose to send to our admissions number. We receive them only when you send that message.

How we use your details

We use enquiry details only to reply about admissions and to contact you about the school. We do not sell your details.

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and links open Google Maps and WhatsApp. These services have their own privacy policies.

Children

Enquiries about children should be sent by a parent or guardian.

Your choices

To ask us to delete an enquiry or to correct details, call 99864 47378.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
