School van: safe travel to and from school

A school van lets children reach school without stress for parents. Ask us whether the van comes to your area in Hassan.

What parents should know

  • Pick-up and drop for children from LKG to Class 10
  • Routes, timings and van fees are shared at admission
  • Parents can call the school to ask about their area

Ask before you apply

Tick “School van needed” on the enquiry form, or call 91481 50388 or WhatsApp 78926 31849, and we will tell you if your area is covered.
