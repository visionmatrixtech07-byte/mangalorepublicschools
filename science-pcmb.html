Science (PCMB) PU course in Hassan

PCMB is the most flexible Science combination at the Pre-University level. With both Mathematics and Biology, it keeps medicine and engineering open at the same time, so students can decide on a career after II PU rather than at 15.

Subjects in PCMB

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, along with language subjects as per the Karnataka Pre-University Education Board scheme. The course runs for two years: I PU and II PU.

Who should choose PCMB

  • Students who want to study medicine, dentistry, AYUSH, nursing, pharmacy, veterinary or agriculture
  • Students who want to keep engineering and pure science open as well
  • Students who like understanding how the body and nature work, along with solving numerical problems

Entrance exams and next steps

  • NEET-UG for MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS and allied courses
  • KCET and JEE Main for engineering and technology
  • CUET-UG for B.Sc and central university programmes

Career paths after PCMB

  • Doctor, dentist, AYUSH practitioner, physiotherapist, nurse
  • B.Pharm, B.Sc Agriculture, Veterinary science, Biotechnology
  • B.E / B.Tech, B.Sc and research careers

Eligibility

Students who have passed SSLC (Class 10) or an equivalent examination are eligible to apply. Please bring your marks card and transfer documents when you visit.

Frequently asked questions

Can I do engineering after PCMB?

Yes. PCMB includes Mathematics, so students can appear for KCET and JEE Main as per the exam rules.

Is PCMB better than PCMC?

Neither is better. Choose PCMB if medicine or life sciences interest you, and PCMC if computers and engineering interest you.

How long is the course?

It is a two year course: I PU and II PU, followed by the board examination at the end of II PU.
