Science (PCMC) PU course in Hassan

PCMC replaces Biology with Computer Science. It suits students who want engineering, computer applications, data and technology careers, and it keeps a strong base in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.

Subjects in PCMC

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science, along with language subjects as per the Karnataka Pre-University Education Board scheme. The course runs for two years: I PU and II PU.

Who should choose PCMC

  • Students who want B.E / B.Tech in Computer Science, AI, data science or electronics
  • Students interested in BCA, B.Sc Computer Science and software careers
  • Students who enjoy logic, programming and problem solving

Entrance exams and next steps

  • KCET for engineering seats in Karnataka
  • JEE Main for national level engineering institutes
  • CUET-UG for B.Sc and other university programmes

Career paths after PCMC

  • Software engineer, app and web developer
  • Data analyst, AI and machine learning engineer, cyber security
  • Core engineering, B.Sc, BCA and higher studies in technology

Eligibility

Students who have passed SSLC (Class 10) or an equivalent examination are eligible to apply. Please bring your marks card and transfer documents when you visit.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take medicine after PCMC?

No. NEET-UG needs Biology, so choose PCMB if medicine is your goal.

Do I need to know programming before joining?

No. Students start from the basics. Interest in logic and Mathematics matters more.

Is PCMC good for engineering?

Yes. It covers Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, the subjects used for KCET and JEE Main.
